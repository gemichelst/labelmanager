🎧 Demos & A&R

The Demos & A&R module is your complete demo management system — from public submission through to a signed deal. It replaces email inboxes and spreadsheets with a structured, visual pipeline.

The A&R Pipeline

All demos move through a 7-stage Kanban board. Drag cards between columns or use the quick-move dropdown on each card.

Pipeline Stage Definitions

StageMeaningTypical action
NewJust received, not yet listened toListen and rate
ReviewedListened to, initial impression notedRate 1–5 stars, add comment
ShortlistStrong contender, worth deeper discussionShare with team, discuss internally
In TalkActive conversation with artist / managerNegotiate terms, request stems
OfferFormal offer sent or in progressDraft contract, await signature
Signed ✓Deal closed, release plannedCreate release record
RejectedNot a fit — polite decline sentOptional: send auto-response

Demo Submission Form

The public submission form lives at /submit (or via the Submit Demo button in the A&R view). You can embed it on your label website as an iframe or share the direct URL.

Submission Form Fields

FieldRequiredNotes
Submitter Name✅The person submitting (may differ from artist name)
Email✅Used for auto-response emails
Track Title✅
Genre—Free text or dropdown (configurable in Settings)
BPM—Numeric, used in demo cards and scoring
Key—E.g. "A minor", "F# major"
Artist (Roster link)—Links submission to existing artist profile
Notes / References—Artist's message, influences, Soundcloud link
Audio File✅MP3, WAV, AIFF, FLAC, M4A — max 50 MB
📨 Auto-response: After submission, the artist receives an automatic confirmation email (configurable in Settings → A&R → Auto-responses). You can customise the subject and body. A separate rejection template is available for when you move a demo to Rejected.

The Demo Detail View

Click any demo card to open the full detail view. It contains:

Waveform Audio Player

Powered by WaveSurfer.js. Click anywhere on the waveform to seek. Keyboard Space to play/pause. The player shows duration, current position and responds to the scrollwheel for volume.

Star Rating

Rate 1–5 stars directly on the Kanban card or in the detail view. Ratings feed into the A&R Scoring Engine (see below). The rating persists per user — different team members can rate independently.

Internal Comments

Add timestamped comments visible only to the team. Supports @mention of team members (they receive a notification). Comments cannot be deleted — only appended — to preserve the full review history.

Status Quick-Change

The status dropdown in the detail view moves the demo to any pipeline stage. Status changes are logged in the activity feed with the user's name and timestamp.

A&R Scoring Engine

The scoring engine automatically calculates a score out of 100 for each demo based on four weighted factors:

FactorWeightSource
Internal star rating (avg. across team)40%Team ratings in app
Artist history on label20%Number of existing releases by linked artist
Pipeline velocity20%Time spent in current stage vs. historical average
External signals20%Social links present, Spotify/Beatport links, follower data

The score is shown as a coloured bar on each Kanban card. Go to A&R → Scoring tab for the full sorted scoring table with filter by genre, score range and pipeline stage.

Watchlist

Artists or demos that aren't ready for signing but look promising can be added to the Watchlist. The Watchlist appears as a separate section in the A&R view. Watchlist entries can have a follow-up date — you'll receive a notification when it's due.

Bulk Actions

Select multiple demos using the checkbox on each card (hold Shift to range-select), then use the bulk action bar that appears at the bottom:

  • Move all selected to a pipeline stage
  • Assign to a team member
  • Delete selected
  • Export selected as CSV
💡 A&R tip: Set up the Shortlist → In Talk automation (Integrations → Automations) to auto-notify your label manager via Slack webhook whenever a demo reaches the Offer stage. This keeps the whole team in sync without manual pinging.
