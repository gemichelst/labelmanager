🎤 Artists & CRM

The CRM is the heart of LabelManager OS. Every artist on your roster and every external contact — manager, booker, PR agent, blog, radio station, distributor — lives here, fully interlinked.

Artist Profiles

Each artist profile is a 360° view of everything connected to that artist: releases, demos, events, royalty history, contracts, media assets and contacts.

Click the artist card or name to open the 360° detail view with tabbed sections: Overview, Releases, Demos, Events, Financials, Contacts, Media, Contracts.

Contacts

The Contacts section manages all external relationships: managers, booking agencies, PR companies, distributors, blogs, radio shows, promoters and more.

Linking Contacts to Artists

In the Artist Detail view, go to the Contacts tab and click + Link Contact. Select the contact, specify the relationship type (e.g. "Manager", "Booking EU", "Booking US") and save. The linked contact then appears on the artist's profile and vice versa.

Interaction History & Notes

In both Artist and Contact detail views, the Activity tab shows a chronological log of:

  • Status changes (e.g. demo moved to Signed)
  • New releases, events and payouts linked to this entity
  • Manual notes added by team members
  • Contract creations and updates

To add a manual note, click the + Add Note button. Notes support @mentions of other team members and are timestamped automatically.

Search & Filter

The Artists list supports:

  • Text search — searches name, bio, genres, email simultaneously
  • Status filter — show only Active / Development / Legacy / Inactive
  • Type filter — Core, Development, External, etc.
  • Sort — by name, release count, demo count, date added
💡 Tip: Use the genre tags consistently (e.g. always "Techno" not "techno" or "TECHNO") — the A&R scoring engine uses genre matching to suggest related demos to existing artists.

Exporting Artist Data

From any artist detail view, click the ⋯ menu → Export to generate:

  • EPK (Electronic Press Kit) — HTML page with bio, stats, releases, social links and player
  • vCard (.vcf) — Import directly into address book
  • JSON — Full artist record for backup or migration
