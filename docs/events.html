Settlement

After the show, open the event and click Settle. The settlement screen lets you record:

  • Actual fee received (may differ from agreed fee)
  • Travel and hotel expenses
  • Other deductions or additions
  • Net payout to artist (auto-calculated)

Confirmed settlements automatically push a payout entry to the Finance module and update the artist's royalty balance in their portal.

Calendar View

The Calendar tab in Events shows all confirmed gigs on a monthly grid. You can filter by artist to see one artist's touring schedule, or view the full label calendar. Export to ICS format (compatible with Google Calendar, Apple Calendar) via the Export → ICS button.

Riders & Documents

Attach PDF or DOCX riders directly to the event. Use the Generate Rider PDF button to create a formatted tech or hospitality rider from the structured fields — useful for quick sharing with promoters.

💡 Automation tip: Set up the Gig Confirmed → Create Itinerary Tasks automation (Settings → Automations) to auto-generate a checklist (visa check, flights booked, hotel confirmed, tech rider sent, advance paid) whenever a booking reaches Confirmed status.
