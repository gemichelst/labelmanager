💰 Finance

The Finance module gives you a complete picture of your label's money — income, expenses, recoupable costs, artist payouts and overall P&L — without needing a separate accounting tool for day-to-day label tracking.

Finance Overview Dashboard

The Finance dashboard shows at a glance:

  • Total Income — sum of all royalty income and other revenue this year
  • Total Expenses — production, marketing, distribution, travel costs
  • Net Balance — income minus expenses
  • Outstanding Payouts — what's owed to artists but not yet paid
  • Recoupment Status — total advances issued vs. recouped per artist

Income Records

Income entries are primarily created by the Royalties module (see Royalties & Splits) when you import distributor CSV reports. You can also add manual income entries for:

  • Licensing fees (sync, broadcast)
  • Merch revenue
  • Bandcamp sales (if not using distributor import)
  • Grant income
  • Other non-royalty revenue

Expenses

Click + New Expense to log a label cost. Each expense can be:

CategoryProduction, Mastering, Artwork, Marketing, Distribution, Travel, Studio, Legal, Other
AmountGross amount in your label currency
VAT / TaxVAT amount for accounting purposes
Linked ReleaseAssign cost to a specific release (for per-release P&L)
Linked ArtistAssign as recoupable cost against an artist's royalty account
RecoupableToggle — if on, this cost reduces the artist's payable balance
DescriptionFree text note
Receipt / InvoiceAttach PDF from Media Library
DatePayment or invoice date

Recoupment Tracking

When an expense is marked as Recoupable and linked to an artist, it reduces that artist's royalty balance. The recoupment status shows:

  • Total recoupable advances/expenses issued to artist
  • Total royalties earned by artist to date
  • Remaining balance to recoup before payouts begin
  • Whether the artist is In Profit or Unrecouped

Payouts

When an artist has a positive balance (royalties earned minus recoupable costs), you can create a payout. Click + New Payout, select the artist, enter the amount and payment method. The system:

  • Deducts the payout from the artist's outstanding balance
  • Records the payment date and method
  • Adds the payout to the artist's portal statement history
  • Fires a payout.created webhook event (if configured)

Per-Release P&L

In the Release detail view → Finance tab, you can see a per-release profit & loss:

  • Total royalty income generated by this release
  • Production and marketing costs assigned to this release
  • Net position (income minus costs)
  • Breakdown by territory and revenue source
📊 Note on accounting: LabelManager OS is a label management tool, not a full accounting system. For VAT filings, corporate tax and statutory accounts, export your data and use it alongside DATEV, Lexoffice or your accountant's tools.
