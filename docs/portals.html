Dedicated Portals

Provide transparent, read-only views for external partners.

Artist Portal

When an Artist logs in, they see a personalized dashboard:

  • Financials: Gross royalties, their split, total paid, and current balance.
  • Statements: Downloadable PDF royalty statements.
  • Music: Status of their current Releases and submitted Demos.
  • Assets: Approved press kits and masters linked to them.

Manager / Agency Portal

When a Manager logs in, they see:

  • Roster: Overview of artists they represent.
  • Gigs: Confirmed bookings and routing.
  • Pitches: Demos currently in the A&R pipeline.
