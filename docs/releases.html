Releases & Campaigns

Manage the lifecycle of your music catalog.

Release Statuses

A release moves through a logical chain: Idea → A&R Selected → In Production → Mastered → Distributor Setup → Pre-Save → Scheduled → Released → Post-Campaign.

Smart Task Templates

When you create a Release, the system generates a timeline of tasks based on the release type:

  • Single: Tasks for streaming pitch, artwork, and social assets.
  • Club EP: Tasks for DJ promo pools and Beatport pitching.
  • Vinyl: Plant booking, test press approvals, distribution logistics.

Tasks calculate their due dates dynamically based on the target Release Date.
