Royalties & Finance

A professional accounting layer for indie labels.

Royalty Contracts

Define the financial relationship between the Label and the Artist. Set the Artist Split %. This engine calculates shares automatically.

Financial Entries

  • Royalties: Income received from DSPs or distributors. Log the Gross amount, and the system calculates the Artist Share.
  • Expenses: Marketing costs, mastering fees, etc. (Can be marked as recoupable).
  • Payouts: Record actual money transferred to the artist to calculate their current Balance Due.

Statements

Generate periodic PDF statements. Once generated, statements are automatically available to the Artist in the Artist Portal.
