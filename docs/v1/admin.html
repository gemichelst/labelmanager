Settings, RBAC & Integrations

Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

Assign roles in the Users tab. Permissions dictate UI visibility:

  • Admin: Full access.
  • Label Manager: Access to everything except critical system settings.
  • A&R: Can manage Demos and view CRM/Releases. No financial access.
  • Artist: Restricted to the Artist Portal.

Integrations

Navigate to the Integrations tab to connect the OS to the outside world.

  • Platform Import: Drop a CSV from LabelRadar, SubmitHub, or Soundplate to bulk-import demos into your pipeline.
  • Distributor Export: Select a Release and generate a delivery package (CSV/XML) for DistroKid, FUGA, Believe, or Bandcamp.
  • Webhooks: Configure outbound webhooks to Slack or Discord. Trigger events on demo.new, release.created, or gig.confirmed.

Notifications

The bell icon in the top right provides an activity feed of new demos, expiring contracts, and due tasks.
