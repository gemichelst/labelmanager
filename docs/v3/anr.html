Demos & A&R

The complete demo pipeline — from public submission form to signed deal. No more demo emails.

📖 10 min read 🕐 Last updated May 2026

The Demos & A&R module is your complete demo management system — from public submission form to signed deal. Demos never land in your email inbox again.

The A&R Pipeline

The pipeline is a 7-stage Kanban board. Each demo card moves left to right as it progresses:

StageMeaning
NewJust submitted — not yet reviewed
ReviewedListened to, initial decision made
ShortlistPotential — saving for deeper consideration
In TalkActive conversation with the artist
OfferDeal terms proposed
Signed ✓Deal done — becomes a release
RejectedNot a fit — archived with feedback

The Submission Form

The public submission form lives at /submit (or as a standalone page you can embed on your label website). It requires no login.

Submission Fields

FieldRequiredNotes
Name✅Artist or submitter name
Email✅For A&R reply
Track Title✅Working title is fine
GenreFree text (e.g. Techno, Acid)
BPMNumeric
Keye.g. A minor, F# major
Artist (roster select)If submitter is already on your roster
Notes / ReferencesDescription, influences, reference tracks
Audio File✅MP3, WAV, AIFF, FLAC, M4A · max 50 MB
💡
Embed the submission form

Copy the /submit URL and link to it from your label website, Instagram bio or Linktree. For full embedding, use an <iframe> pointing to the form page.

Reviewing Demos

Waveform Audio Player

Every demo card with an uploaded audio file shows an inline waveform player powered by WaveSurfer.js. You can:

  • Click anywhere on the waveform to scrub to that position
  • Play / pause with the ▶ button or Space
  • See the track's BPM and key metadata inline on the card

Star Ratings

Rate each demo 1–5 stars directly on the Kanban card. Ratings are saved instantly and used by the A&R Scoring Engine to prioritise your queue.

Moving a Demo Through the Pipeline

Two ways to move a demo:

  • Quick-move dropdown — the small select box on each Kanban card
  • Demo Detail view — open the demo, use the status dropdown in the header

Demo Detail View

Click any demo card title to open the full detail view. This shows:

  • Full waveform player with the uploaded audio file
  • All metadata: genre, BPM, key, submitter email, submission date
  • Status selector and star rating
  • Notes from the submitter
  • Internal comments thread (team-only)
  • Linked artist profile (if on roster)

Internal Comments

In the Demo Detail view, the comments panel allows your team to discuss the demo privately. Comments support @mentions and are timestamped. The submitter never sees these comments.

To add a comment, type in the comment field and press Enter or click the send button.

A&R Scoring Engine

The scoring engine automatically calculates a priority score for each demo based on four weighted factors:

FactorWeightSource
Internal star rating40%Your team's rating (1–5 stars)
Artist history on label20%Previous releases, demo history
Time in pipeline20%Longer in pipeline = lower priority
External signals20%Social links, platform presence provided by submitter

The score is shown as a small indicator on each card. Filter the Kanban by score to prioritise the highest-potential demos first.

Watchlist

Use the Watchlist tab to track artists you're interested in but aren't ready to sign yet. Watchlisted artists receive periodic reminders (manual) to re-submit, and you can track their external stats over time.

Bulk Actions

Select multiple demo cards by holding Shift and clicking, then use the bulk action bar:

  • Move all to a different stage
  • Send standard rejection email to all selected
  • Export selected demos as a PDF or CSV report
  • Archive / delete
⚠️
Audio files in file:// mode

Audio playback and file uploads require the app to be served via HTTP, not opened directly as a file. Use python3 -m http.server 8080 or VS Code Live Server.
