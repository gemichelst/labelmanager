Artists & CRM

Manage your entire label roster and every external contact — managers, bookers, PR, distributors, blogs and more.

📖 8 min read 🕐 Last updated May 2026

The CRM is the heart of LabelManager OS. Every artist on your roster and every external contact — manager, booker, PR agent, blog, radio station, distributor — lives here, fully interlinked.

Artist Profiles

Each artist profile is a 360° view of everything connected to that artist: releases, demos, events, royalty history, contracts, media assets and contacts.

Creating an Artist

1

Click + New Artist in the Artists tab

The artist creation modal opens with all fields.

2

Fill in the Artist Profile form

FieldDescription
Name *Stage name / artist alias
Real NameLegal name for contracts and royalty statements
TypeCore Artist, Development, Legacy, External, Compilation-only
StatusActive, Development, Inactive, Legacy
GenresComma-separated tags (e.g. Techno, Industrial, EBM)
TerritoryPrimary territory for rights and distribution
BioFull artist biography (shown on EPK)
Short BioOne-liner for press releases
EmailDirect artist contact
PhoneDirect phone / WhatsApp
Booking EmailBooking enquiries email
Social LinksSpotify, SoundCloud, Beatport, RA, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube
Contract InfoFree-text notes about deal structure (superseded by Royalties contracts)
Internal NotesPrivate label notes — not visible in Artist Portal
3

Save and open the full Artist Detail view

Click the artist card or name to open the 360° detail view with tabbed sections: Overview, Releases, Demos, Events, Financials, Contacts, Media, Contracts.

Artist Status Types

StatusMeaning

LegacyNo longer active but catalogue still managed
InactiveSuspended or deal ended

Contacts

The Contacts section manages all external relationships: managers, booking agencies, PR companies, distributors, blogs, radio shows, promoters and more.

Blog / PlaylistEditorial contacts, playlist curators

Linking Contacts to Artists

In the Artist Detail view, go to the Contacts tab and click + Link Contact. Select the contact, specify the relationship type (e.g. "Manager", "Booking EU", "Booking US") and save.

Interaction History & Notes

In both Artist and Contact detail views, the Activity tab shows a chronological log of status changes, new releases and events linked to this entity, manual notes added by team members, and contract updates.

To add a manual note, click + Add Note. Notes support @mentions of other team members and are timestamped automatically.

Search & Filter

The Artists list supports:

  • Text search — searches name, bio, genres, email simultaneously
  • Status filter — show only Active / Development / Legacy / Inactive
  • Type filter — Core, Development, External, etc.
  • Sort — by name, release count, demo count, date added
💡
Consistent genre tags

Use genre tags consistently (e.g. always "Techno" not "techno" or "TECHNO"). The A&R scoring engine uses genre matching to suggest related demos to existing artists.

Exporting Artist Data

From any artist detail view, click the ⋯ menu → Export to generate:

  • EPK (Electronic Press Kit) — HTML page with bio, stats, releases, social links and player
  • vCard (.vcf) — Import directly into address book or phone
  • JSON — Full artist record for backup or migration
